Favorites

Star up to 10 streamers in the popup. They stay at the top of the list, and their live status is checked automatically every hour (and right when you star someone).
Hourly checks can't see a stream that starts and ends between two checks. Clicking a notification opens their Kick channel.

Match overview

Possible impersonators appear in the popup with a ⚠ warning, below your other matches. Use “Not them” or “It's them” on a row to correct one we got wrong.

Match corrections

Kick channels are matched by username, so a few will be a different person. Matches you've marked “not them” or “it's them” in the popup or on a Twitch page are listed here. Your corrections always win over the automatic result, and “Clear cache” doesn't remove them.

To back these up (together with your favorites and settings), use the Backup tab.

Backup

Save your match corrections, favorites and settings to a file, and restore them later: after removing and re-adding the extension, loading it from a different folder, or on another computer (each of those starts with empty storage). “Clear cache” doesn't remove any of this.

Included: match corrections, favorites, and your display, checking-speed and notification settings.
Not included: API keys and secrets (a backup file is easy to share by accident), your Twitch and Kick logins (just log in again), and cached lookups (the popup re-checks your follows the first time, as on a new install).

When you import, you'll see what will change first. Corrections are merged with yours (the file wins for the same streamer). Favorites and settings in the file replace your current ones. Older corrections-only exports still work.

Diagnostics

What the extension knows about itself. No tokens or secrets are shown or copied.
